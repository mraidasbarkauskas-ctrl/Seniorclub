Wondering how to help your loved one?

Suomen Senior Club ry exists to promote the wellbeing of older adults and their loved ones. This page is for you — an adult child, a spouse, or another close person — looking to find the right kind of support or meaningful activity for an ageing family member or friend.

Does any of this sound familiar?

Many people contact us precisely because they're worried about someone else — not because they need help themselves. These situations may sound familiar:

  • Your loved one lives alone and rarely sees other people.
  • You've noticed that memory or everyday coping isn't quite what it used to be.
  • Your loved one seems low, withdrawn, or lonely.
  • You live far away and aren't sure who to reach out to locally.
  • You'd like to help them find meaningful activities and new friends — but don't know where to start.

You're in the right place, even if you can't yet put your finger on exactly what's needed. Below are a few directions that are often a good place to start.

Where to find help

A few places to start

MUMILI

If your loved one would enjoy company and meaningful activity around memory, mind and movement — a free club for ages 60+.

Seniors' Friendship Club

If your loved one feels lonely and would welcome a warm, safe group and new friends.

Seniors' Fitness

If you'd like to find a safe, regular way to stay active and meet others together.

Seniors' Mind

Personal, confidential conversation support for older adults — and, when needed, for their loved ones too.

When it affects you too

Worries about a loved one's ageing, memory, or loneliness don't only affect the older person — they often weigh on family and friends as well. Seniors' Mind offers free, confidential conversation support not only to older adults, but to their loved ones too. You don't have to carry your worries alone, and it's completely fine to seek support for yourself as well.

Not sure where to start?

That's alright — call or write to us, and we'll work out together what group or kind of support might fit your situation.
