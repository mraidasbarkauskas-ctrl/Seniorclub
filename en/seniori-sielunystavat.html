Seniors' Friendship Club

Joy to the soul and happiness to the heart.

Hello! We are Janetta and Aidas, the facilitators of the Seniors' Friendship Club

We invite everyone who feels lonely or lacks warm and heartfelt communication. Here, you will meet people with similar goals and concerns. Many face the same challenges as you. You will gain an invaluable gift — the opportunity to share life with other people.

In a cozy and safe environment, you can express your feelings and experiences, receive support, share your dreams, and gain the determination to realize them. You will try new things, create, and share your experiences, transforming your life for the better.

Who is this group for?

  • For those who value well-being, safe communication, and support.
  • For those looking to make new friends.
  • For those seeking emotional and physical relief, learning to release stress and anxiety.
  • For those who wish to receive attention, warmth, and care.

Friends make good times better and bad times easier. Join us and find a warm-hearted community where everyone feels valued.
