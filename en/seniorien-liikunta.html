Seniors' Fitness

Welcome to free exercise and wellbeing activities for people aged 60 and over! Seniors' Fitness for energy, longevity and a sharp memory.

Programme includes

  • Gentle exercise — mobility, muscle strength and coordination
  • Balance and fall prevention
  • Adapted martial arts exercises for memory and concentration
  • Self-defence basics and safe everyday action
  • Qigong and Asahi for breathing and overall wellbeing

Our sessions help you

  • Feel strong and confident
  • Build strength, reaction speed, coordination and mobility
  • Learn to keep your balance and fall safely (if you slip or trip)
  • Improve your memory and reduce the risk of dementia through a special set of movements (kata)
  • Learn to protect yourself and your loved ones

Participation is free and open to everyone. Groups are aimed especially at multilingual seniors aged 60 and over.

Instructor

Sessions are led by Aidas Barkauskas, European Karate Champion, who has received training in working with older adults and has experience leading senior fitness groups.
