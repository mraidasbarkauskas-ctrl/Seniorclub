Senior Samurai

Who says you can't be strong and mobile in old age? A fitness and martial-arts group for energy, longevity and a sharp memory.

Feel the energy of martial arts

Physical activity, fitness, self-defence basics for seniors and senior karate — led by a European Champion! It's a great way to stay in good physical shape. Senior karate develops every muscle group, coordination, and the ability to manage your own state. If you value health, activity and energy, and dream of a long, fulfilling life, discover strengths and possibilities you couldn't have imagined yesterday.

Our sessions help you

  • Feel strong and confident.
  • Build strength, reaction speed, coordination and flexibility.
  • Learn to keep your balance and fall safely (if you slip or trip).
  • Improve your memory and significantly reduce the risk of dementia through a special set of movements (kata).
  • Learn to protect yourself and your loved ones.

Instructor: Aidas Barkauskas, European Karate Champion.
