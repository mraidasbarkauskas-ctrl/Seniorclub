About usSuomen Senior Club ry

Who we are and why we do this work

Suomen Senior Club ry is a registered association that supports the well-being of older people and their families — in Finnish, English and Russian.

Our purpose

Suomen Senior Club ry aims to promote the mental and physical well-being of seniors, enhance their longevity, prevent memory loss and cognitive-related diseases, combat loneliness, and support emotional well-being. We offer assistance and support in Finnish, Russian and English.

Our approach

Low-threshold, multilingual support

We work in a practical, hands-on way: low-threshold group activities, individual conversational support, and project work that reaches both Finnish-speaking and foreign-language older people in the Helsinki metropolitan area. You can take part in Finnish, English or Russian, and many of our groups are designed especially for those who are not yet familiar with Finnish or the Finnish service system.

You can find our full range of activities — clubs, projects and individual support services — on the homepage and in the schedule.
